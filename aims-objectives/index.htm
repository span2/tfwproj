Aims & Objectives

Background

The Core Valley Lines (CVL) network runs services from Cardiff Queen Street to Cardiff Bay, Coryton, Treherbert, Aberdare, Merthyr Tydfil and Rhymney, operated by Transport for Wales (TfW). There are also freight routes to Hirwaun (beyond Aberdare) and Cwmbargoed (branched from Rhymney line at Ystrad Mynach), which are currently mothballed, but require maintenance. These stations, along with the area of legislative control (as defined by TfW) are shown in Figure 1.

The CVL network plays an important role for the local community and beyond. From the 2021 UK Census data, it shows approximately 1.5% of residents from Rhondda Cynon Taff, Caerphilly and Merthyr Tydfil are currently using the CVL trains to commune to work, with 2.5% of residents using buses. The CVL provide the key transport links into and beyond the towns and villages along the valleys. Furthermore, over 65% of the residents are currently driving to work. Improving the reliability of the rail network will have a huge potential to make their transport in the region more sustainable for the population of a region increasingly under threat of climate change. This will align well with Welsh Government’s “The Clean Air Plan for Wales – Healthy Air, Healthy Wales (2020)” as its commitment and long-term ambition to improve air quality in Wales, and particularly relevant to “Creating sustainable places through better planning, infrastructure and transport”.

Aims

  • Assess the vulnerability of the Core Valley Lines (CVLs) Network in Cardiff region operated by Transport Wales (TfW) under the climate change conditions
  • Design and establish a flood warning / decision support system for the study site

Objectives

  • Assess fully the impacts of climate change on the CVL services and transport infrastructures through the numerical modelling and field monitoring;
  • Quantify the vulnerability and risks of the railway system;
  • Set up and acquire data from new field measuring/monitoring stations for water levels, flow velocity and discharges, and weather parameters at locations along River Cynon and River Taff
  • Build and test an operational flood risk warning / decision support system to provide evidence-based guidance for long-term management, planning and investment for the CVL, as well as the effective mitigation to the potential flood risk.
